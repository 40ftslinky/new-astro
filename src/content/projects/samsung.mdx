### Brief:

Samsung is a leader in defence-ready mobile devices and wearables, with proven deployments around the globe. We were asked to help promote their purpose-built software solution for the Australian Defence Force and forward deployed emergency workers. The solution was designed to enhance situational awareness, improve decision-making, and increase operational efficiency in high-stress environments.

### Solution:

How do you authentically photograph and film the use of technology that is highly classified and operationally inaccessible?

With no opportunity for live production, we built the entire creative system using approved source materials from NATO and Five Eyes partners. The result was a compelling, credible visual narrative that accurately represented real-world defence public safety and emergency services use cases.
