### Brief:

Addison Road Community Centre is a vibrant hub for community engagement, offering a range of programs and services to support local residents. We were tasked to create visually compelling printed materials that effectively communicated the centre's mission, values, and activities to the community.

### Solution:

During the projects, we developed a range of printed documents covering various styles and content including annual reports, educational and historical publications. The designs incorporated the centre's branding elements, creating a cohesive visual identity across all printed materials.
