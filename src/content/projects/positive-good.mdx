<h2>Brief:</h2>

<p>After establishing a long term relationship with Positive Good, we were tasked to update the brand and design and develop a refreshed online presence.</p>

<h2>Solution:</h2>

<p>The brand update reinforces the agency's direct, human-first approach to new business. The overall aesthetic signals that Positive Good is not a conventional agency — it's a purpose-driven partner – fluent in both strategy and storytelling. Large type and bold visuals create a strong, memorable presence that communicates the brand's values effectively. Avoiding the earthy tones of the traditional energy industry - Positive Good's updated visual language expresses a bright, loud expression that I belive is a challenge to the future of clean energy.</p>
