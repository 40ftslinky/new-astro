## Overview

<p>Custom digital and print assets for electoral education campaigns for Protect Our Winters, an environmental advocacy organization focused on combating climate change. </p>

## Brief

<p>We were tasked with creating a suite of digital and print assets to support Protect Our Winters for issue specific election campaign education to reflect the broader message of protecting the places we love. The goal was to develop engaging and informative content that would resonate with voters and encourage them to support candidates who prioritize climate action.</p>
