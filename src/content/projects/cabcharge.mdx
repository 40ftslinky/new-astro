<h2>Brief:</h2>

<p>Cabcharge required a campaign specific landing page to encourage customers to calculate possible savings when using their services .</p> 

<h2>Solution:</h2>

<p>A brand appropriate landing page was designed and developed to be used as a campaign specific destination for paid media traffic. The page included a savings calculator, which allowed users to input their current taxi spend and see how much they could save by switching to Cabcharge.</p>
<p>A standard marketing funnel was implemented, with a clear call to action to encourage users to sign up for Cabcharge's services. The page was designed to be visually appealing and easy to navigate, with a focus on the benefits of using Cabcharge.</p>

<p class="small">Web development, Programmatic marketing, UX/UI design</p>
