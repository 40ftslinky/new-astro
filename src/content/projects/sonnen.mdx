### Brief:
Sonnen's entrance into the Australian market required a comprehensive suite of digital and video assets to introduce their innovative energy storage solutions. The project aimed to create engaging content that highlighted Sonnen's commitment to sustainability, clean energy, and smart home integration. 

{/* is a leading provider of smart energy storage solutions, offering innovative products that enable homeowners and businesses to generate, store, and manage their own renewable energy. The company is committed to sustainability and reducing carbon emissions through the use of clean energy technologies. */}

### Solution:
Over several product launch campaigns, we developed a range of digital and video assets that effectively communicated Sonnen's brand values and product offerings. This included creating promotional videos, social media content, and web assets that showcased the benefits of Sonnen's energy storage solutions. The content was designed to resonate with environmentally conscious consumers and position Sonnen as a leader in the renewable energy sector in Australia.
