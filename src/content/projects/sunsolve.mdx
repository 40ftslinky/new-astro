## Overview

<p>PV Lighthouse needed a coherent brand and product architecture for the advanced, physics-driven software platform that creates digital twins of solar cells, modules, and full-scale photovoltaic systems they had developed . </p>

## Solution

<p>A modern, professional brand identity was developed around a distinctive illustrative brandmark, an energetic colour palette, and clear, consistent typography. Together, these elements reflect the company’s expertise in advanced solar software while presenting its services and values as bold, trustworthy, and accessible. The identity was applied across digital and print touchpoints, including a new website, marketing collateral, and brand guide, ensuring consistency across every communication.</p>
